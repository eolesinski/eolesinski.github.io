I am a Master of Computer Science student focusing on software systems and fintech applications. I am also the author and maintainer of [PoliSciPy](https://poliscipy.github.io/poliscipy/), an open-source Python package for U.S. election visualization.

Most recently, I was a software engineering intern at [Morgan Stanley](https://www.morganstanley.com), where I worked on improving trading infrastructure for the fixed income trading desk. Previously, I also interned at the [MIT Digital Currency Initiative](https://www.dci.mit.edu) and [Federal Reserve](https://www.bostonfed.org), where I worked on various projects related to Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs).

Prior to graduate school, I received my Bachelor of Science in Economics and Minor in Mathematics from the [University of Nevada, Reno](https://www.unr.edu). I also spent a year as an undergraduate Visiting Student at [Columbia University](https://www.columbia.edu) studying financial economics and computer science.

_Research Interests: Digital Currency • Distributed Systems • Public Policy_
